Mixing Lenses & Cross-Alchemy

Combine Red, Yellow, and Blue into Orange, Purple, and Green beams, or intersect two beams on a single tile to forge Pink, Gold-Brown, and Cyan crystals.

🪞

90° Tilt Mirrors & Splitters

Tap mirrors directly on the board to bend laser beams 90° around corners, or use Prism Splitters to divide a single beam into two directions at once.

🌊

Dynamic 3-Wave Progression

Every level flows seamlessly through 3 waves where hit target rings pop with tactile animations and make way for fresh optical puzzles.
