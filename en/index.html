🔮 NEW GAME • OPTICAL COLOR & LASER PUZZLE

Kromatik

Mix primary gems, tilt mirrors 90° & conquer 5 crystal worlds

Kromatik is a relaxing yet clever logic puzzle game of color alchemy and light refraction. Place primary gems (Red, Yellow, and Blue) side by side on the board to form optical mixing lenses, shoot Orange, Purple, and Green laser beams across the grid, and solve every stage in 3 dynamic waves!

🌊 3 Dynamic Waves Per Level
🪞 90° Mirrors & Prism Splitters

Our Philosophy

Why people love using our apps

🛡️

Privacy by Design

All our apps work 100% offline. No accounts, no mandatory sign-ups, no analytics, and zero selling of personal data. Your device is your vault.

🎨

Every App Its Own Style

We don't believe in a single rigid template. Whether it's a clean minimalist utility, a psychological decision tool, or a vibrant game: every concept gets its own tailored visual language and feel.

⚡

Fast & Reliable

No sluggish loading screens or bloated webviews. We craft native, responsive apps that run smoothly on any device, even in airplane mode.
