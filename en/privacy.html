1. Gutzy (com.gutzy) — Data & 100% Offline Operation

None. Gutzy is engineered from the ground up around Privacy by Design:

  • No user accounts: You never need to create a profile, sign in, or provide an email address to use the full application.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or any third-party ad networks.
  • Zero data sharing or selling: Because we do not collect any data, no personal information is ever sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All decisions, dilemmas, journal notes, reflections, and preferences are stored exclusively locally in the memory of your own device (using your mobile operating system's standard sandboxed local storage).

  • This information never leaves your smartphone or tablet at any time.
  • If you uninstall the application from your device, all locally saved decisions and preferences are permanently deleted.

3. App Permissions

Gutzy does not request access to any sensitive sensors or data on your device:

  • No access to contacts or address book.
  • No access to location or GPS data.
  • No access to camera or microphone.
  • The app only uses vibration (haptic feedback) for the tactile experience when flipping the coin or shaking the Magic 8-Ball.

4. Children's Privacy (Gutzy: PEGI 3 / Everyone)

Because Gutzy collects zero personal information and contains no third-party ads or external trackers, the application is completely safe and suitable for users of all ages.

5. Plinkzy (com.waie.dropzy) — Specific Privacy Terms

Plinkzy (package ID: com.waie.dropzy) is an arcade coin-drop and pinball game. The following specific terms apply to Plinkzy:

  • No Account Required & Local Storage: Plinkzy does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. All gameplay progress (high scores, Vault Gold, unlocked stages, and themes) is stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. You can permanently erase all locally stored game data at any time directly inside the app via Menu ➔ Privacy & Data ➔ Clear All Local Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Optional Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob): On mobile devices (Android and iOS), Plinkzy uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, when you choose to watch an ad to receive bonus drops or double gold). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and device-level information, including device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), coarse/approximate location (derived from IP address) for ad relevance and fraud prevention, and app interaction/ad performance diagnostics. All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's system settings (Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads). Learn more at: Google Partner Sites Policy.
  • No Real-Money Gambling: All coins, fiches, jackpots, and Vault Gold in Plinkzy are strictly virtual, in-game arcade points with zero real-world monetary value. They cannot be purchased for real money or exchanged/cashed out for real money or prizes.
  • Target Audience (Plinkzy): Plinkzy is intended for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Kromatik (com.waie.kromatik) — Specific Privacy Terms & Anonymous Player Database

Kromatik (package ID: com.waie.kromatik) is an optical color and laser puzzle game. The following specific privacy and data processing terms apply to Kromatik (in accordance with the EU GDPR):

  • No Personal Account Required: Kromatik does not require mandatory registration and never asks for your real name, email address, phone number, home address, or password.
  • Anonymous Player Database & Cloud Synchronization (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore eur3): To safeguard your gameplay progress and enable cross-device restoration without mandatory registration, Kromatik automatically generates a random, anonymous Player ID (playerUid, e.g., kr_...). Your gameplay data is stored locally on your device (SharedPreferences / localStorage) and synchronized under this anonymous playerUid to our European Google Firebase Cloud Firestore database (region eur3 — European Union). The anonymously stored fields consist strictly of:
    • In-game profile preferences: Your chosen in-game nickname (playerName), selected avatar icon (avatar), language preference (language), and active board/laser skin (activeSkin);
    • Gameplay progress: Highest level reached (highestLevel), number of completed levels (levelsCompleted), current world (currentWorld), total earned stars (totalStars), experience points (xp), and perfect level streak (perfectStreak);
    • Virtual balances & entitlements: Balance of Prism Crystals 💎 (crystals), available boosters (hintsLeft, novaLeft / 2× Ring Poppers, and breakerLeft / Mine Breakers), Royal Pass / PRO unlock status (isPro), and customer-support crystal grants (adminGiftCrystals);
    • Technical metadata: Application version (appVersion), platform type (android_app or web_browser), and timestamp of last synchronization (lastActiveIso).
  • Anonymous Game Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): We use Google Firebase Analytics on an aggregated and anonymized level to understand level progression, optimize puzzle difficulty and balance, monitor app stability, and measure aggregate in-app purchase conversion events.
  • Optional Rewarded Video Advertising (Google AdMob with UMP Consent): Kromatik never interrupts puzzles with forced pop-up ads. Players may voluntarily choose to watch short rewarded video advertisements (via Google AdMob, including the Google UMP GDPR consent form in the EEA/UK) to earn free Prism Crystals, hints, or extra moves. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), coarse location (derived from IP address) for ad relevance and fraud prevention, and ad performance diagnostics over encrypted HTTPS connections. With the Royal Pass / PRO upgrade or when you do not start rewarded videos, no ads are shown. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads).
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): Optional in-app purchases (such as the Royal Pass / PRO, Power Bundle, or Prism Crystal Packs) are processed exclusively and directly through the secure payment system of the Google Play Store (Google Play Billing). WAIE Studio never receives or stores your payment card details.
  • Your GDPR Rights (Access, Portability & Deletion): We never sell data to third parties. You can view and copy your anonymous Player ID (playerUid) at any time directly inside the app (via the Player Profile or Settings ➔ Privacy Policy & Terms) to restore your progress on another device. Furthermore, you can request access to or permanent deletion of your anonymous player profile from our cloud database at any time by emailing info@waie.be quoting your Player ID (playerUid).
