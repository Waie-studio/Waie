Privacy Policy

Applicable to Gutzy (com.gutzy) & Plinkzy (com.waie.dropzy) • Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications Gutzy (com.gutzy, including earlier test versions under the working title Decido) and Plinkzy (com.waie.dropzy, formerly known under the working title Dropzy) and all related services provided by WAIE Studio ("we", "our", or "the developer").

In short: We never require user accounts and never sell personal data. All progress, decisions, and settings are stored locally on your own device.

1. Gutzy (com.gutzy) — Data & 100% Offline Operation

None. Gutzy is engineered from the ground up around Privacy by Design:

  • No user accounts: You never need to create a profile, sign in, or provide an email address to use the full application.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or any third-party ad networks.
  • Zero data sharing or selling: Because we do not collect any data, no personal information is ever sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All decisions, dilemmas, journal notes, reflections, and preferences are stored exclusively locally in the memory of your own device (using your mobile operating system's standard sandboxed local storage).

  • This information never leaves your smartphone or tablet at any time.
  • If you uninstall the application from your device, all locally saved decisions and preferences are permanently deleted.

3. App Permissions

Gutzy does not request access to any sensitive sensors or data on your device:

  • No access to contacts or address book.
  • No access to location or GPS data.
  • No access to camera or microphone.
  • The app only uses vibration (haptic feedback) for the tactile experience when flipping the coin or shaking the Magic 8-Ball.

4. Children's Privacy (Gutzy: PEGI 3 / Everyone)

Because Gutzy collects zero personal information and contains no third-party ads or external trackers, the application is completely safe and suitable for users of all ages.

5. Plinkzy (com.waie.dropzy) — Specific Privacy Terms

Plinkzy (package ID: com.waie.dropzy) is an arcade coin-drop and pinball game. The following specific terms apply to Plinkzy:

  • No Account Required & Local Storage: Plinkzy does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. All gameplay progress (high scores, Vault Gold, unlocked stages, and themes) is stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. You can permanently erase all locally stored game data at any time directly inside the app via Menu ➔ Privacy & Data ➔ Clear All Local Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Optional Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob): On mobile devices (Android and iOS), Plinkzy uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, when you choose to watch an ad to receive bonus drops or double gold). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and device-level information, including device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), coarse/approximate location (derived from IP address) for ad relevance and fraud prevention, and app interaction/ad performance diagnostics. All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's system settings (Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads). Learn more at: Google Partner Sites Policy.
  • No Real-Money Gambling: All coins, fiches, jackpots, and Vault Gold in Plinkzy are strictly virtual, in-game arcade points with zero real-world monetary value. They cannot be purchased for real money or exchanged/cashed out for real money or prizes.
  • Target Audience (Plinkzy): Plinkzy is intended for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
